Simple 4-Step Process

How It Works

Transforming your water bottle into a wearable sling takes less than 5 seconds. No buckles, no clips, no separate accessories.

1

Grab your bottle

Your WearBottle starts as a sleek, minimalist reusable bottle. The label sits flat and seamless against the cylindrical body.

2

Peel the label

Lift the tactile corner tab. The biomimetic micro-suction strap peels away smoothly with zero sticky glue or gummy residue.

3

Turn it into a sling

Unfurl the reinforced strap. The integrated anchor grips loop securely around the bottle neck and base, locking your bottle firmly in place.

4

Wear and enjoy

Sling it cross-body over your shoulder or neck. Move freely with both hands open while your water stays conveniently at your hip.
